How to play

Unscramble the letters to spell the hidden word. Click letters to build your answer, then hit Check.

Levels

LevelTime limitPoints per word
Easy2 minutes10 pts
Medium3 minutes20 pts
Hard4 minutes30 pts

Get 5 correct in a row to advance to the next level.

Lives

You have 3 lives ❤️. Each wrong answer costs one life. Lose all three and the game ends.

Timer

Each level has its own countdown. If time runs out, the game ends. The timer resets when you advance to the next level.

Hints

You start with 5 free hints. Using a hint reveals a clue for the current word. You can also buy additional hints for −5 pts each once your free hints are gone.

⭐ Any unused free hints are worth +100 pts each as a bonus at the end of the game — so use them wisely!

Skips

You have 2 skips per game. Use a skip to move on to a new word without losing a life or breaking your streak.
